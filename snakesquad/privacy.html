Snake Squad privacy policy

Effective 23 October 2026

Snake Squad is an Android maze shooter game by Blueline Studio. This policy explains what happens to information when you play it. In short: Snake Squad has no accounts and no servers of its own, and the developer collects no information that identifies you. The game shows ads from Google AdMob and measures how the game is played with Google Analytics for Firebase; both are described below.

Information the game keeps on your phone only

  • Your progress and settings (level, coins, upgrades, music, sound and vibration settings, whether you bought "Remove ads") are saved in a file on your phone. The developer never receives it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Purchases

"Remove ads" is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Google Play. Google Play processes the payment under Google's privacy policy; Snake Squad never sees your payment details. The game asks Google Play, on your phone, whether your Google account owns "Remove ads", so it can restore it on a new phone.

Ads

Snake Squad shows ads from Google AdMob: videos you choose to watch (to revive or to double your coins) and an occasional full-screen ad between levels. "Remove ads" turns off the ads between levels; videos you choose to watch stay available. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ads software in the game may collect and share with Google:

  • your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate its general location;
  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID;
  • how you interact with ads (for example views and taps) and app launches;
  • diagnostic information about the game's and the ads software's performance.

How Google uses this information is explained in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's advertising policies.

Game analytics

Snake Squad uses Google Analytics for Firebase to learn how the game is played, for example which levels players reach, how long sessions last, and how often players come back, so the developer can fix difficulty and bugs. It collects an app instance ID, your device's advertising ID, device model and Android version, approximate location from your IP address, and in-game events such as starting and finishing a level. The developer sees only totals and trends, never your name or contact details. Google processes this information under Firebase's privacy information.

Your choices

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and some US states), Snake Squad shows Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You can change your choices at any time from the pause screen under Privacy options. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Android's settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Google > Ads on some phones).

All information sent to Google is encrypted in transit.

Children

Snake Squad is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect information from children.

Keeping and deleting information

The developer keeps no information that identifies you, so there is nothing for the developer to delete. Uninstalling Snake Squad removes everything it saved on your phone. Information collected by Google for ads and analytics is kept and deleted under Google's policies linked above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
