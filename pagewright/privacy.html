Pagewright privacy policy

Effective 12 November 2026

Pagewright is an Android app, by Blueline Studio, for merging, splitting, organizing, compressing, converting, protecting and signing PDF files. This policy explains what happens to information when you use it. In short: your PDFs never leave your phone. Pagewright does all of its PDF work on your device, has no accounts and no servers of its own, and the developer collects no personal information. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some information as described below.

Your files

The files you open, their contents, their names and any passwords you type are processed on your phone and are never uploaded, sent or shared, by the app or by its ads. Pagewright never changes the file you opened; it always writes a new file where you choose to save it.

Information the app keeps on your phone only

  • Working copies of files while a tool runs. They are deleted when you finish, and any left behind are cleaned up within 24 hours.
  • Your saved signatures (up to 3), a list of recently saved files, and an error log you can view or clear in Settings.
  • Your choices in the app, such as theme and default quality, and whether you own the ad-free unlock.

None of this is sent anywhere. The app's data is excluded from Android backups and device-to-device transfers, and uninstalling Pagewright removes all of it.

Purchases

Removing ads is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Google Play. Google Play processes the payment under Google's privacy policy; Pagewright never sees your payment details. The app asks Google Play, on your phone, whether your Google account owns the unlock, so it can turn ads off and restore the unlock on a new phone.

Ads in the free version

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home and result screens, and an occasional full-screen ad between a finished task and its result. No ads, and no ad software, run during your first 3 finished tasks, and ads never appear in the middle of a task. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ads software in the app may collect and share with Google:

  • your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate its general location;
  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID;
  • how you interact with ads (for example views and taps) and app launches;
  • diagnostic information about the app's and the ads software's performance.

This information is encrypted in transit. Nothing from your files goes into an ad request: no file names, contents or tool settings. How Google uses this information is explained in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's advertising policies.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and some US states), Pagewright shows Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You can change your choices at any time in the app under Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Android's settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Google > Ads on some phones).

Removing ads stops all of this

If you buy the ad-free unlock, Google's ads software never starts again, and Pagewright makes no network connections of its own.

Children

Pagewright is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect information from children.

Keeping and deleting information

The developer keeps no information about you, so there is nothing for the developer to delete. Uninstalling Pagewright removes everything it saved on your phone. Information collected by Google for ads is kept and deleted under Google's policies linked above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
